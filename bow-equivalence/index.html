How to read this

Equivalence compares average weapon damage, before attributes, skills, off-weapon ED, Crit/DS, enemy resistance or hit chance. Windforce’s +20 Dexterity, +20 Strength, mana leech, knockback and procs are excluded. Windforce has 40 innate IAS and a native ED roll of 275–350%; higher figures require corruption/socket bonuses. Required ED can exceed obtainable item rolls.

This comparison uses damage per hit only. Attack speed, attributes, skills, Crit/DS and enemy resistance are not included. Bows cannot normally be ethereal.
