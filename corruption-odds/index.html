Calculation details & rules

Supported: magic, rare, crafted, set and unique weapons, helms, body armour and shields. Regular/white items, jewelry, maps and desecration are outside this calculator.

Recipe thresholds come from the supplied S14 patch 2 CubeMain.txt. The calculation enumerates the 1–1000 corruption factor and follows the first matching outcome row. Socket results are capped by base and item-level socket limits; rolls above the cap accumulate at the cap.

A socket slam cannot also gain an ED/IAS slam. To combine a modifier with sockets, use a Puzzlebox/Puzzlepiece before or after corrupting. Puzzling gives 1–2 sockets on one-handed weapons/armour, or 2–4 on two-handed weapons/bows/crossbows, capped by the base. Existing sockets cannot be increased. The correct puzzle item for the quality is assumed available.

Optional minimum ED/IAS values apply only to the corruption's added rolls, not the item's original stats. Numerical ranges are modelled as uniform integer rolls. Other modifier rolls are unrestricted. No spending, attempt counts or trading assumptions are included.

Recipe-based probabilities, not a live-game sample or exhaustive RNG-seed audit. Special item exceptions and current server changes outside the supplied files may differ. PD2 corruption reference.
