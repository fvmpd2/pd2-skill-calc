How Rabies and Creeper work

Rabies: infection ≠ poison

A successful bite starts a 5-second infection. Its invisible missiles repeatedly apply poison and spread the remaining infection time. A late infection does not start a new full 5-second outbreak.

More bites do not add independent poison stacks. Faster attacks help land and spread bites; they do not speed up poison ticks. Weapon damage, Crit/DS, Crushing Blow and leech belong to the bite, not its poison.

The controller is configured to emit every frame. Actual spread depends on collisions, distance, living carriers and time remaining; this is not one guaranteed hit per frame.

Why −enemy poison res still matters

It raises poison damage per frame on each application. Reapplication does not cancel that advantage. Its poison-length benefit is less simple: a new application can replace the previous timer, while all carriers retain the original infection deadline.

The primary results therefore show DPS. The single-application totals below are not a simulation of every reapplication during an outbreak.

Creeper: coverage, not triple DPS

Up to three creepers lay poison trails. More creepers improve coverage and debuff uptime; their poison is not multiplied by summon count. Their resistance debuff does not stack with itself.

Trail lifetime: 4 seconds. Base poison duration: 4 seconds. The trail hit-delay parameter is 15 frames (0.6 seconds), not the poison tick rate. Both poisons deal damage at 25 frames per second.
